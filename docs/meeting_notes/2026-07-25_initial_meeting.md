## Project Overview

Develop a reproducible bioinformatics pipeline for identifying and visualising integron cassette arrays in *Vibrio* genomes.

### Objectives

- Evaluate existing integron detection software.
- Compare cassette identification and reconstruction methods.
- Develop a reproducible and modular analysis pipeline.

---

## Why *Vibrio*?

- Focus on chromosomal superintegrons.
- Large cassette arrays make them suitable for studying integron organisation and evolution.

---

## Initial Tasks

- Read key literature on integrons and superintegrons.
- Identify existing integron detection tools.
- Compare strengths and limitations of available software.
- Design the pipeline workflow.

---

## Future Direction

Potential future analyses include:

- Cassette rearrangement
- Cassette gain and loss
- Horizontal gene transfer
- Evolution of chromosomal integrons
- Comparison with mathematical model predictions
